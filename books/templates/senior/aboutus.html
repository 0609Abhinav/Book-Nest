About BookNest

India's trusted platform for pre-owned book exchange and discovery

Our Mission

BookNest is an online venture to serve pre-owned books throughout India. We are India's first community-driven marketplace for second-hand books. We partner with multiple second-hand retailers and markets around various cities, expanding our inventory and ease of access.

We deal with the educational sector including School, College, University — and the recreational sector including Novels and activity-oriented books for kids. Our aim is to bridge the gap between people looking for used books and those willing to share them.

Meet the Founder

Abhinav Tripathi

Founder & Developer

Abhinav is passionate about making quality education accessible to everyone. With a strong technical background in software development, he built BookNest to connect readers and help students access affordable books across India.

Our Story

We observed a lot of books going to waste and eventually started collecting and sharing them. Starting from a small collection, we built BookNest to create a digital platform where everyone can discover, share, and download books — anywhere in India.

Our Operations

We believe education's prime support is books. We take steps to distribute books to financially weak students. We welcome schools and colleges to contact us for partnerships and book drives.
