About BookNest

India's trusted platform for pre-owned book exchange and discovery. Read, share, and connect.

Our Mission

BookNest is an online venture to serve pre-owned books throughout India. We are India's first community-driven marketplace for second-hand books. We partner with multiple second-hand retailers and markets around various cities, expanding our inventory and ease of access.

We deal with the educational sector including School, College, University — and the recreational sector including Novels and activity-oriented books for kids. Our aim is to bridge the gap between people looking for used books and those willing to share them.
